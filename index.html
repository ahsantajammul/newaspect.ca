The Story Behind the Lens

A Passion That
Found Its Purpose

"I've always seen the world a little differently — in colour, in symmetry, in the quiet details most people walk right past."

I've had an eye for the world around me for as long as I can remember. Long before I picked up a camera professionally, I found beauty in the details — the way light falls across a room, the texture of an autumn leaf, the unguarded smile of a child.

After moving from Pakistan to Canada with my family, I channelled that lifelong sensitivity into something real. What started as a deep curiosity about visual storytelling quickly grew into a thriving business. I began with real estate — helping agents and homeowners show their properties in their best light — and things took off from there.

Today, I cover everything from drone aerials and interior staging to portraits, family sessions, brand content, and events. I bring the same intention to every shoot: to see things as they truly are, and make them unforgettable.

— Alina
Founder, New Aspect Photography
